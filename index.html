The trip to Atlanta, Georgia is where Justin Bieber’s career officially exploded, transforming him from a viral YouTube kid into a major industry prospect. Scooter Braun flew a 13-year-old Justin and his mother, Pattie Mallette, out to Atlanta free of charge to record demo tapes.
While at an Atlanta recording studio, a legendary chance encounter occurred in the parking lot.
Usher pulled up in his car just as Justin and Scooter were walking in.
Recognizing the R&B superstar, a starstruck Justin ran up to him and said, "Usher, I love your songs, do you want me to sing one of them to you?" Usher didn't realize who the kid was, brushed him off politely because it was cold outside, and told him, "Let’s just go inside, little buddy," essentially passing on the offer.
Usher wasn't the only one who noticed. Pop superstar Justin Timberlake also caught wind of Bieber and tried aggressively to sign him to his own label
This triggered a massive, high-profile music industry bidding war between Usher and Timberlake.
Timberlake offered major incentives, but Usher ultimately won the battle by making a deeply personal pitch to Scooter and Justin’s mom:

"If you go with Justin Timberlake, that’s Justin and Justin. With me, there is only one Justin."
